# VGU Buddy Knowledge File: Vivekananda Global University (VGU), Jaipur

Collected from vgu.ac.in on 25 October 2026.

This file contains only what VGU's official website states; where the site contradicts itself, each version is given with a short note.

## About VGU

Vivekananda Global University (VGU) is a university in Jaipur, Rajasthan. Its About page says VGU has existed "since its inception in 2012", is sponsored by the Bagaria Education Trust and is named after Swami Vivekananda. It describes "over 33,834+ students" from nearly every Indian state and union territory and "more than 23+ countries". (Source: https://www.vgu.ac.in/who-we-are/vivekananda-global-university)

VGU is NAAC A+ accredited and recognised by ICAR (Indian Council of Agricultural Research), and the homepage FAQ says it "is recognized by UGC". The About page gives its QS Asia ranking as 666th in Asia, 200th in South Asia and 95th in India. Note: that page labels the ranking "Asia 2026" in its main text but "Asia 2025" in its highlights box. (Source: https://www.vgu.ac.in/who-we-are/vivekananda-global-university; https://www.vgu.ac.in/)

The homepage also lists "Ranked 25th In BBA by Times B School", "Ranked 3rd — Outlook iCare Ranking", "GU MEI Ranking 2025" and "IIRF Education Impact Award 2026". Note on NIRF: the Admissions page says "Top 100 in NIRF" while the Placement Overview page says "NIRF Ranked #25 in India"; these differ, give no year, and the homepage rankings list does not mention NIRF. (Source: https://www.vgu.ac.in/; https://www.vgu.ac.in/admission; https://www.vgu.ac.in/placements/overview)

The homepage states "200+ Programs Available" (UG, PG, PhD and Integrated) and a "31+ Acre Campus". Note: the General Facilities page says "33-acre campus", and the Programs page says "14 Faculties" and "50+ Programs". (Source: https://www.vgu.ac.in/; https://www.vgu.ac.in/campus-facilities/general-facilities-and-services; https://www.vgu.ac.in/programs)

The Office of the President page names Prof. (Dr.) D.V.S. Bhagavanulu as President of VGU. The Leadership Speaks page lists Dr. Lalit K. Panwar, IAS (Retd.) as Chairperson, Dr. K.R. Bagaria as Founder & Vice Chairperson and Dr. K. Ram, IPS (Retd.) as Chief Patron. Note: the R&D Cell page's Research Board table lists Prof. (Dr.) N.D. Mathur as President and Prof. (Dr.) DVS Bhagavanulu as Pro-President. (Source: https://www.vgu.ac.in/academic-leadership/office-of-president; https://www.vgu.ac.in/who-we-are/leadership-speaks; https://www.vgu.ac.in/research/research-and-development-cell)

## Programmes: B.Tech

B.Tech is run by the Faculty of Engineering & Technology (Dean: Prof. (Dr.) Baldev Singh), with departments of Computer Science and Engineering, Electrical, Mechanical and Civil Engineering. Its B.Tech programmes are Civil, Mechanical, Electrical, Computer Science and Engineering (CSE), CSE (Artificial Intelligence), Artificial Intelligence & Data Science, CSE with IBM certification, Computer Science & Technology (SELECT), CSE (Internet of Things & Cyber Security), CSE (Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning), Cloud Computing and Software Engineering. The CSE department page also lists B.Tech Computer Engineering And Application and B.Tech Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning, and shows every B.Tech as 4 years. (Source: https://www.vgu.ac.in/engineering; https://www.vgu.ac.in/engineering/computer-science-engineering)

B.Tech eligibility is "10+2 with PCM / relevant subjects, min. 45%", with admission via VGUCET / JEE Main. Lateral entry, B.Tech (LEEP), takes 3 years and needs 45% marks (40% SC/ST) in a Diploma in Engineering, B.Sc or B.Voc. (Source: https://www.vgu.ac.in/admission/ug-admission)

Annual B.Tech fees for 2026–27 (course total in brackets) are: Mechanical / Civil / Electrical ₹1,50,000 (₹6.00 L); CSE ₹2,00,000 (₹8.00 L); CSE (AI / Cloud Computing / IoT & Cyber Security / AI & ML) and CSE with IBM Certification ₹2,15,000 (₹8.60 L); CST — SELECT ₹2,75,000 (₹11.00 L); Software Engineering ₹2,50,000 (₹10.00 L); and LEEP ₹1,50,000 (₹4.50 L). The UG Admission page lists B.Tech AI & Data Science at ₹2,15,000 a year. (Source: https://www.vgu.ac.in/admission/fee-structure; https://www.vgu.ac.in/admission/ug-admission)

The CSE department groups its specialisations as "Artificial Intelligence, Data Science & Machine Learning", Full Stack & Software Development and "Cloud Computing, IoT & Cyber Security". It describes B.Tech AI & Data Science as "Artificial Intelligence, ML, Deep Learning", and the Engineering page says the CSE curriculum is "designed with IBM and NIAT". CSE labs include Programming, DBMS & Data Analytics, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Cyber Security, Cloud Computing and Web Development labs. (Source: https://www.vgu.ac.in/engineering/computer-science-engineering; https://www.vgu.ac.in/engineering)

The CSE page claims "500+ recruiters", packages "reaching up to 1 Cr PA" and a "95%+ placement rate". The Engineering page says VGU is "Ranked 896th globally in Engineering & Technology" with "AIR 111 in the Outlook Best College Rankings", and that Mechanical students train in the "Mitsubishi Industrial Robotics Center". (Source: https://www.vgu.ac.in/engineering/computer-science-engineering; https://www.vgu.ac.in/engineering)

M.Tech (2 years, ₹1,05,000 a year) is offered in CSE, Robotics, AI&ML, Power System, Production, Structural, Environmental, Thermal and Transportation Engineering. Ph.D.s are offered in CSE, Mechanical, Electrical and Civil Engineering. (Source: https://www.vgu.ac.in/engineering; https://www.vgu.ac.in/admission/fee-structure)

## Programmes: B.Sc.

Science B.Sc. programmes belong to the Faculty of Basic & Applied Sciences (FBAS), whose page names Prof. (Dr.) Mridula Purohit as Dean (note: the R&D Cell's Research Board lists Prof. (Dr.) Kailash Agrawal as Dean and her as Associate Dean). FBAS offers B.Sc. / B.Sc. (H) in Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics (PCM); Chemistry, Botany, Zoology (CBZ); Information Technology; and Forensic Science (General, Criminology, Cyber Forensic). It describes these as "NEP-aligned 4-Years B.Sc Degrees". (Source: https://www.vgu.ac.in/basic-applied-sciences; https://www.vgu.ac.in/research/research-and-development-cell)

B.Sc in IT, CBZ or PCM takes 3 or 4 years, needs 10+2 with PCM / PCB / Biotech / Biology as compulsory subjects, and costs ₹60,000 a year. (Source: https://www.vgu.ac.in/admission/ug-admission)

FBAS also offers 2-year M.Sc. programmes in Forensic Science (₹1,10,000 a year) and in Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Botany and Zoology (₹60,000 a year). Ph.D.s are offered in Physics, Chemistry, Botany, Zoology, Microbiology, Biochemistry, Environmental Science, Mathematics and Biotechnology. (Source: https://www.vgu.ac.in/basic-applied-sciences; https://www.vgu.ac.in/admission/fee-structure)

B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture (ICAR recognised) takes 4 years, costs ₹1,10,000 a year, and needs 10+2 with Agriculture or Physics / Chemistry / Mathematics / Biotech / Biology with at least 45%; "JET Appearing is Must". The Programs page also lists B.Sc HHM (Hospitality) and B.Sc Optometry (Allied Healthcare). The Faculty of Computer Science & Applications lists BCA and MCA, not B.Sc. programmes. (Source: https://www.vgu.ac.in/admission/ug-admission; https://www.vgu.ac.in/programs; https://www.vgu.ac.in/computer-science-and-application)

## Admissions

For 2026–27, UG admission is "via VGUCET / JEE / NATA / CLAT / JET"; VGUCET is VGU's own entrance test, and the homepage says VGU "also accepts JEE, CAT, CUET". PG admission accepts CAT / GATE / MAT / VGUCET. (Source: https://www.vgu.ac.in/admission/ug-admission; https://www.vgu.ac.in/; https://www.vgu.ac.in/admission)

VGUCET is a free, 60-minute online exam taken from home (webcam-proctored), covering English Proficiency, Logical Reasoning, Quantitative Aptitude and General Awareness, with no negative marking. Results come within 7 working days, and the score decides the scholarship percentage. The site's VGU-CET links go to https://forms.vgu.ac.in/cet. (Source: https://www.vgu.ac.in/admission)

For the 2026–27 cycle, the Admissions page lists Phase I exams April 1 – May 10, 2026 (also shown as Apr 1 – May 09 on the same page), Phase II June 1 – June 30, 2026, last date to apply June 30, 2026, and spot admission in July 2026. The UG Admission page gives a reporting date of 24th July 2026 and says the session starts August 2026. (Source: https://www.vgu.ac.in/admission; https://www.vgu.ac.in/admission/ug-admission)

Documents needed are Class 10th and 12th certificates and marksheets, a transfer / migration certificate, Aadhaar or other government photo ID, 2×2-inch photographs, an entrance score card (optional) and a caste certificate if claiming reservation. ABC ID / APAAR ID creation is mandatory for all students. (Source: https://www.vgu.ac.in/admission/ug-admission)

Note: the Admissions page's own eligibility table sometimes differs from the UG Admission page (for example, 50% minimum for BBA). It also says SC/ST/OBC/PWD candidates get a 5% relaxation in minimum marks. (Source: https://www.vgu.ac.in/admission)

## Scholarships

The Scholarship page (titled "2025–26") gives merit scholarships on qualifying-exam marks: 15% for 90% onwards and 10% for 75% onwards. Current VGU students and alumni get 20%, VGU-group employees with 2+ years' service get 20%, and medalists in sports, theatre, dance, performing arts, NCC or NSS get 5% (district), 10% (state, or cluster / zonal CBSE) or 15% (national). The CET / CUET scholarship is decided after the exam result. (Source: https://www.vgu.ac.in/admission/scholarship)

Scholarships are applied in the even semester only, continue only with a minimum 6 SGPA each semester, and are revoked if a student is detained for attendance shortage. They are only for Indian students, only on the academic fee, and only one category can be used. Queries go to admissions@vgu.ac.in. (Source: https://www.vgu.ac.in/admission/scholarship)

The homepage and Fee Structure page advertise "Scholarships up to 100%". The Admissions page ("VGU Scholarship 2026–27") gives VGUCET bands for UG programs: 90%+ gets 100%, 80–89% gets 75%, 70–79% gets 50%, 60–69% gets 25% and 50–59% gets 15%; for B.Tech, JEE rank below 50,000 gets up to 75% and below 10,000 up to 100%. Note: that page's renewal rule (7.5 CGPA, 75% attendance) and extra categories differ from the Scholarship page, and the site does not reconcile them. (Source: https://www.vgu.ac.in/admission/fee-structure; https://www.vgu.ac.in/admission; https://www.vgu.ac.in/admission/scholarship)

Scholarships cover tuition only (not hostel, mess or transport) and do not apply to Ph.D. programmes. Note: the Clubs page shows a 20% "Co-curricular Scholarship", unlike the 5–15% medalist rates. (Source: https://www.vgu.ac.in/admission/fee-structure; https://www.vgu.ac.in/campus-life/clubs)

## Fees

Fee Structure 2026–27 charges are: Application Form ₹2,000; Enrolment ₹10,000 (non-refundable); Caution Money ₹10,000 (refundable); Registration ₹2,500, Library ₹1,000 and Sports & Culture ₹1,000 per semester; and Alumni ₹7,500 and Degree / Convocation ₹7,500 once. The examination fee is notified separately each year. (Source: https://www.vgu.ac.in/admission/fee-structure)

Tuition is paid through the university ERP in two equal instalments, due 1st June (odd semester) and 15th December (even semester). Students unpaid 20 days after the semester starts are de-registered and must pay ₹11,000 re-registration charges. The Ph.D. fee is ₹1,55,000 a year (3 years minimum). (Source: https://www.vgu.ac.in/admission/fee-structure)

Note: the Admissions page lists different "indicative" fees, such as B.Tech ₹1,20,000 – ₹1,40,000 a year, Ph.D. "₹60,000/year" and a ₹5,000 registration fee, and says final fees are confirmed at admission. It also gives a refund schedule: 100% (minus ₹1,000) if withdrawing 15+ days before the session, 80% if less than 15 days before, 50% within 30 days after the start, 25% at 31–60 days, and none after 60 days. (Source: https://www.vgu.ac.in/admission)

## Hostels

VGU's on-campus hostels have 2,590+ beds (280 girls' rooms and 650 boys' rooms), separate for boys and girls, with 24×7 security, CCTV, biometric access at key entry points and 100 Mbps Wi-Fi. Hostel admission is part of the university admission process. (Source: https://www.vgu.ac.in/campus-life/hostels; https://www.vgu.ac.in/)

Annual hostel fees are: Triple ₹1,00,000; Double ₹1,25,000; Single ₹1,35,000; Double with attached bathroom ₹1,45,000; Single with attached bathroom ₹1,55,000; AC Double ₹1,55,000; AC Single ₹1,65,000; AC Double with attached bathroom ₹1,70,000; and AC Single with attached bathroom ₹1,80,000. AC electricity is billed separately by meter reading. (Source: https://www.vgu.ac.in/campus-life/hostels)

Extra charges are a ₹10,000 refundable security deposit, ₹35,000 non-refundable room booking (opens 10th May, except for 1st-semester students) and ₹10,000 a year for laundry; fees are paid on 1st June and 15th December. Note: the Admissions page lists different hostel fees, such as AC Double Sharing ₹1,15,000. (Source: https://www.vgu.ac.in/campus-life/hostels; https://www.vgu.ac.in/admission)

Hostellers use the Zolo Scholar App for room management, mess bookings and complaints. Facilities include a gym with male and female trainers, laundry, an ATM near Gate No. 2, a free cloakroom for semester breaks and a dispensary with a doctor on call 24×7. (Source: https://www.vgu.ac.in/campus-life/hostels)

The Students' Mess serves 7,000+ meals a day in six dining spaces seating about 1,500, with vegetarian food daily, Jain food and special diets on request; the Admissions page says the campus canteen serves vegetarian and non-vegetarian food. Mess timings are breakfast 07:00–08:00 AM, lunch 11:30 AM–01:30 PM, snacks 04:30–05:15 PM and dinner 07:30–09:00 PM. ## Campus facilities

VGU has about 90 lecture theatres, 22 tutorial rooms, 25 computer programming labs, 90 teaching and research labs, seminar halls and a Computer Centre, all Wi-Fi enabled, across the Academic, Technology and Administrative Blocks. There are also moot courts, digital studios, drawing halls and audio-video rooms. (Source: https://www.vgu.ac.in/campus-facilities/academic-facilities)

Library services are two Knowledge Resource Centres, in the Academic Block and the Technology Block, with books, journals, magazines and IT resources; that page gives no book count. The Admissions page describes a "Central Library" with 2 lakh+ books and 10,000+ e-journals. (Source: https://www.vgu.ac.in/campus-facilities/academic-facilities; https://www.vgu.ac.in/admission)

The Medical Aid Centre in the Students' Mess Complex gives free consultation to students, faculty and staff, with a doctor holding OPD hours on a schedule. The campus also has physiotherapy and occupational therapy OPD, eye check-up and blood testing, and a 24/7 ambulance.
