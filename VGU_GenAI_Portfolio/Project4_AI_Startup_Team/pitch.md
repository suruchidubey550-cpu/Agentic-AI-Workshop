# LostOnCampus
*Find What's Lost. Stay What's Real.*

Students lose items daily, especially in high-traffic areas. LostOnCampus helps them find lost items via map-based searches and real-time alerts sent through the student app. Now, with rising scam deliveries, it also flags suspicious package drops using verified campus location data.

## Plan
- **Problem**: Students lose items frequently on campus, leading to stress and frustration.  
- **Users**: VGU students, especially those in large campuses with high foot traffic.  
- **Key Features**:  
  - Upload item photos with location tags (e.g., library, dorms).  
  - Map-based search to find items near users.  
  - Real-time alerts when an item is reported or found nearby.
